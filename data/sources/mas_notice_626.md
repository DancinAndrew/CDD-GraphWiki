# MAS Notice 626: Prevention of Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism

## Section 6: Customer Due Diligence
A bank shall not establish business relations or undertake a transaction for a customer if the bank is unable to complete the CDD measures required under this Section.

### 6.1 General CDD Requirements
A bank shall perform Customer Due Diligence measures in the following circumstances:
(a) When the bank establishes business relations with a customer.
(b) When the bank undertakes any occasional transaction for a customer:
  (i) That is equal to or exceeds SGD 5,000; or
  (ii) That is a wire transfer, regardless of the transaction amount.
(c) When there is suspicion of money laundering or terrorism financing.
(d) When the bank has doubts about the veracity or adequacy of any information or documentation previously obtained.

### 6.2 Enhanced Due Diligence
A bank shall perform Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) measures in respect of any customer who:
(a) Is identified as presenting a high risk of money laundering or terrorism financing.
(b) Is a Politically Exposed Person (PEP), a family member or a close associate of a PEP.
(c) In respect of whom there are complex or unusually large transactions, or unusual patterns of transactions, which have no apparent economic or lawful purpose.

#### 6.2.1 High Risk Measures
For high-risk customers, a bank shall perform the following measures:
(i) Obtain approval from senior management to establish or continue the business relationship.
(ii) Take reasonable measures to establish the customer’s source of wealth and source of funds.
(iii) Conduct enhanced ongoing monitoring of the business relationship.
