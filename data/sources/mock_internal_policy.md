# Global Bank AML and KYC Compliance Policy

## Section 1: Customer Onboarding Standards
Global Bank requires all corporate and individual customers to undergo standard Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures.

### 1.1 Customer Identification
Every customer must be identified and verified before onboarding.
(a) For individuals: Full name, date of birth, residential address, and unique national identification number must be collected and verified against an independent passport or identity card.
(b) For corporate entities: Legal entity name, registration number, registered address, and the names of all active directors must be verified against official registry extracts.

### 1.2 Beneficial Ownership Verification
Global Bank shall identify and verify the identity of the Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBO) of all corporate entities.
(a) The bank shall apply an ownership threshold of 10% or more (which is stricter than the regulatory baseline of 25%) to identify UBOs.
(b) In cases where the ownership structure is complex, including three or more layers of intermediary holding companies:
  (i) The corporate entity is automatically classified as presenting a high risk.
  (ii) Full ownership charts detailing each layer must be signed off by a compliance officer.

### 1.3 High-Risk Reviews and Escalations
High-risk customers, including Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and entities with unclear beneficial ownership, are subject to mandatory periodic reviews.
(a) High-risk customer files MUST be reviewed every 12 months (annual review) to ensure compliance documentation is up-to-date.
(b) If a review reveals significant discrepancies or failure to verify a UBO, the case must be escalated to the Group AML Committee within 5 business days.
